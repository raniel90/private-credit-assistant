Portfolio Alpha is a fictional private credit fund. Its risk policy limits
single-name exposure to 7 percent of net asset value. For this simplified lab,
single-name means one borrower, and exposure is outstanding loan principal.
A risk officer must review an exception before a trade is approved. In this
policy, a trade includes a proposed loan disbursement. Review does not itself
authorize the loan. This fictional policy is demonstration material and does
not describe any real institution's policies.
